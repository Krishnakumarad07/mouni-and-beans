The Story Behind Mouni & Beans

A humble obsession with botanical origins, precision temperatures, and bringing community together.

Established in 2021

Where Heart Meets The Perfect Roast

Founded by coffee connoisseurs, Mouni & Beans started as a cozy pop-up stall with a single vintage espresso machine and a dream: to serve coffee that honors the meticulous craft of coffee planters and roasters.

Today, Mouni & Beans has flourished into a beloved urban sanctuary. We treat every roast profile like fine chemistry and art — dialing in grind sizes, water mineral balance, and roasting curves to accentuate the terroir of every bean.
